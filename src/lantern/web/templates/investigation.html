What the grades and fingerprints mean

Grade is how reliable the website usually is (the Admiralty scale), from a fixed list, not from AI:

  • A Completely reliable: official records.
  • B Usually reliable: governments, international bodies, established news, universities.
  • C Fairly reliable: reference works such as Wikipedia.
  • D Not usually reliable: open platforms and social media. Useful first-hand, unverified alone.
  • F Cannot be judged: not on Lantern's list. Unknown, not necessarily bad.

sha256 is a fingerprint of the saved copy. Change one character and it changes completely, so it proves the evidence was not altered.

Credibility (on each claim, 1–6): 1 confirmed by three or more independent websites, 2 probably true (two), 3 possibly true (one), 4 doubtful (contradicted), 6 not assessed.
